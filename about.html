About Us

Family-owned, right on Union Street.

A little New York glamour, built and run by a family that calls Olean home.

Our Story

Proudly women-, Latino-, Asian-, and veteran-owned.

New Yorks Spa & Nail is a family business first. We recently remodeled the salon top to bottom — new marble floors, new stations, new energy — because Union Street deserves a nail bar that feels like a night out, not a chore.

Whether you're in for a ten-minute polish change or a full spa afternoon with your mom and sisters, our technicians treat every chair like the only one that matters that day.
